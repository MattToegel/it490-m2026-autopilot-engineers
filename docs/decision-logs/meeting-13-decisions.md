## Decision D-066 - Assign Complete User Stories to Individual Owners

## Date:

July 14, 2026

## Status:

Accepted

## Decision:

The team will assign complete user-story responsibilities to individual members rather than splitting every user story into multiple small subtasks across the entire team.

## Rationale:

Splitting every story among multiple people could create confusion, duplicated work, and unclear ownership. Giving an individual ownership of a complete story provides clearer responsibility and makes progress easier to track.

## Consequences:

Each assigned member is responsible for completing the required work associated with their selected user story, coordinating any cross-domain dependencies, and providing the necessary code and evidence for the MVP submission.

## Related Vote:

Accepted by the team during Group Meeting 13.

## Decision D-070 - Keep Project VMs Available During MVP Integration

## Date:

July 14, 2026

## Status:

Accepted

## Decision:

The team will keep the required project VMs running and reachable during the days leading up to the MVP deadline.

The MVP contains cross-domain functionality that depends on communication between the App, RabbitMQ, API Worker, and Database VMs. If one VM is unavailable, another team member may be unable to test or complete their work.
